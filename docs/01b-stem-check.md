# AirStreet STEM check

Verdict: the working principle of the current design, option A, sits within known physics, chemistry and biology, and no row fails outright. Four details failed on first check and were fixed in this session under Amish's standing instruction of 2026-10-23 (AST-DDR-005): the NO2 noise term was below the sensor data sheet (S10), the ozone filter of the NO2 cell can be used up before the cell's two-year replacement (S14), readings outside the sensors' rated ranges were not marked (S15), and the model drew a thicker band than the calculation used (S24). One more row failed on first check and went to Amish because its fixes change the user-facing scope: the NO2 cell is rated only to 40 degC and 85 % RH, while the node is built for 50 degC and humid nights (S33). On 2026-10-30 he chose option (a): accept the flagged hours now and seek a cell rated above 40 degC with the partner later (AST-DEC-001 decision 3, AST-DDR-006), so S33 is restated as the flagging claim and is Plausible. Twelve rows of option A are plausible, to confirm at TRL 4 or in FieldNode, among them the field NO2 accuracy (S11, now about 4.3 ppb hourly after the independent review), the PM humidity correction (S16), the shield's radiation error (S17), the band at its worm slots (S23), the insert pull-out (S26) and the sampling of the hourly means (S34). So `stem_check` is **open**. Of 42 claims across the three options, 29 are within known science, 13 are plausible and none fails; options B and C have no failing row. Method: STANDARDS section 20. Calculations: `docs/04-calcs/stem_check.py`, which runs `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py` (AST-CAL-001 v0.8) for every option A number.

## Fields not relevant

Every field is touched. Biology enters only through the problem statement (S27) and insects in the pod (S31): AirStreet gives indicative pollutant levels and makes no health claim (AST-PRC-001, Safety).

## Concept options

- Option A, electrochemical and optical node on FieldNode (current design, AST-OPT-001): the rows S1 to S35; four fixes made, twelve rows plausible (S33, validity above 40 degC, restated after Amish's decision 3 of 2026-10-30).
- Option B, aspirated, heated-inlet sampling node: within known science on energy (B1), heating (B2) and drying (B3); heating the sample loses part of the semi-volatile nitrate in the particles (B4, plausible), a known effect of heated inlets.
- Option C, passive diffusion tubes with a battery PM logger: within known science; Palmes tubes collect about 1.5 ug of NO2 a month (C1) and give monthly means within about 25 % (C2), and four D cells run the PM logger for a month (C3). Its limit is that it gives monthly NO2 means only, which is a requirement shortfall (AST-OPT-001), not a science failure.
