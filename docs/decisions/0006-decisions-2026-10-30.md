# 0006: Decisions of 2026-10-30

- **Date:** 2026-10-30
- **Status:** accepted

## Context

After a report listing the proposed calls in four repos, Amish wrote on 2026-10-30: "on the calls - i go with your recommendations". The five open decisions of AST-DEC-001 each carried a stated recommendation, so each is recorded below as decided, with the option recommended.

## What changed in the design (decision 2)

The hood hangs under the cross arm between the pod and the shield, on two M5 screws into heat-set inserts, like the pod and the shield cap. The arm keeps its 415 mm length; two more 5.5 mm holes are drilled in its flat leg. Tubes are not part of the node (a laboratory supplies them with analysis); the hood has three sockets and each tube is held by a printed clip.

| Quantity | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parts in the D3 model of option A | 195 (103 fasteners) | 205 (109 fasteners); mass in `cad/fidelity.json` 3.4 kg |
| Mass on the pole | 3.61 kg (3.77 kg with the sun shield) | 3.70 kg (3.86 kg); margin to 4.0 kg 0.14 kg |
| Frontal area | 0.139 m2 | 0.144 m2 (limit 0.15 m2) |
| Wind at 35 m/s | 125 N, 416 N m | 129 N, 431 N m |
| Cross arm stress | 4.4 MPa | 5.1 MPa |
| Sensor head cost | USD 302.50 | USD 306.00 (line 12 USD 2.50 and line 11 USD 1.00, both estimates) |
| Whole node with FieldNode | USD 433.50 | USD 437.00 |

`budget_usd` stays at 285. PR-015 is over the value-engineering target by USD 21.00 (it was USD 17.50).

## Consequences

- The tube change every two to four weeks is a new reason to climb to the pole (hazard H9). It is the partner's visit plan to settle (DEC action 8); the hood is fitted at only the anchor node and one node in five.
- Frontal area has 0.006 m2 of margin and mass 0.14 kg; any further addition to the node needs a trade.
- Decision 3 (b) and the partner agreement for the tubes are actions for Amish, not open decisions.
